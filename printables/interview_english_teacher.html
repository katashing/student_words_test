一、自我介绍与个人信息

1. What is your name?
My name is Wu Ruoyang. My English name is WRyan. You can call me WRyan if you wish.
2. How old are you?
I am nine years old.
3. Where do you study now? / Which school do you go to?
I study at Hanlin Primary School. I am in Grade Three.
4. Do you have a nickname? / What can I call you?
My nickname is Qiqi.

二、家庭与生活

1. How many people are there in your family?
There are four people in my family: my dad, my mum, my brother, and me.
2. Who takes care of your studies at home? / Who helps you with your homework?
My parents help me with my homework.
3. What do you like to do with your family on weekends?
I like to watch TV with my family on weekends.
4. What did you do last Sunday?
Last Sunday, I went to the park with my family. We flew a kite and had a picnic. I played on the slide, and it was a lot of fun.
5. What do you do with your brother?
My brother and I like to play basketball together on weekends. Sometimes, we also watch cartoons and laugh a lot.

三、兴趣与爱好

1. What is your hobby? / What do you like to do in your free time?
My hobby is riding a bike.
2. Do you like sports? / What sport do you like?
I like swimming because swimming is good for my health.
3. Do you like drawing / singing / dancing?
I like drawing, but I don't like singing or dancing.
4. What is your favorite toy? Why?
My favorite toy is my toy car because it is my birthday gift.
5. What is your favourite season? Why?
My favourite season is summer. I like it because the weather is hot and sunny. I can go swimming with my dad, and I can eat ice cream.
6. What is your favourite food?
My favourite food is pizza. I like it because it is cheesy and yummy. I usually eat it with my friends on weekends.

四、学习与阅读

1. What is your favorite subject? Why?
My favorite subject is English because English is useful. We can talk to people around the world in English.
2. Do you like reading?
Yes, I do. My favorite book is Journey to the West.
3. What is your favorite book?
My favorite book is Journey to the West. The Monkey King fights monsters. He is very brave. I like him very much.

五、学校与朋友

1. Why would you choose our school?
I want to come to your school because it is very famous. I love reading, and I know that your school has a big library. I will study hard and be a good student here.
2. What do you do during recess?
During recess, I like to go to the library or stay in the classroom. I read storybooks or draw pictures with my friends. Both places are quiet, and I feel relaxed there.
3. Who is your best friend? Why do you like him?
My best friend is Peter. He is very kind and friendly. I like him because we always play football together at recess. He also helps me when I have problems.

六、品格与情景应对

1. If you see a classmate fall down on the playground, what will you do?
If I see a classmate fall down, I will run over to help. I will ask, "Are you OK?" Then I will tell the teacher.

看图说话 5 · Roy and the Angry Whale

姓名：________________　日期：________________

Look at the pictures and tell a story about Roy and the angry whale. Think of an ending.

Words you may use: threw rubbish · blamed · sunbathing · beach · wore sunglasses · hit his fin · was hurt badly · was frightened · said sorry · was only a dream · promised · be good to the ocean

参考讲述 · Roy and the Angry Whale

以下为一种可能的讲述，结局可合理想象。

One day, Roy threw some rubbish into the sea. Later, he went to the beach, put on his sunglasses and fell asleep in the sun. An angry whale appeared. Its fin was hurt, and Roy was frightened. The whale showed Roy that the rubbish had hurt it. Roy realised that throwing rubbish into the sea could harm sea animals.

Possible ending (one example): Roy suddenly woke up and realised it was only a dream. He picked up the rubbish on the beach and promised to keep the sea clean.

口头提问 · Roy and the Angry Whale

请在学生完成朗读或讲述后逐题提问，不提前展示问题。

  1. What did Roy do at the beginning?
    He threw rubbish into the sea.
  2. How did Roy feel when he saw the whale?
    He felt frightened.
  3. Why was the whale angry?
    The rubbish had hurt its fin.
  4. What could Roy do next?
    Accept a sensible ending, such as saying sorry, clearing up rubbish and promising to protect the sea.

Sample story only. There is no single correct answer. Accept a sensible story and a reasonable ending.

看图说话 8 · Albert Goes to the Cinema

姓名：________________　日期：________________

Albert went to the cinema this afternoon. You are Albert. Look at the pictures and tell the story. Think of an ending.

参考讲述 · Albert Goes to the Cinema

以下为一种可能的讲述，结局可合理想象。

This afternoon, I went to the cinema with Aunt Karen to watch Super Joe. We arrived at three o'clock, but there was a long queue at the ticket counter. We waited until four o'clock. Then Aunt Karen bought some popcorn and drinks. During the film, I suddenly felt unwell and held my stomach.

Possible ending (one example): I told Aunt Karen that I had a stomach ache. She took me outside to rest and asked a member of staff for help. I felt better after a while, but we decided to go home.

口头提问 · Albert Goes to the Cinema

请在学生完成朗读或讲述后逐题提问，不提前展示问题。

  1. Who went to the cinema with Albert?
    Aunt Karen went with him.
  2. Which film did they watch?
    They watched Super Joe.
  3. How long did they wait at the ticket counter?
    They waited for one hour, from three o'clock to four o'clock.
  4. What did they buy at the snack counter?
    They bought popcorn and drinks.
  5. How did Albert feel during the film?
    He felt unwell and seemed to have a stomach ache.
  6. What could Aunt Karen do next?
    Accept a reasonable ending, such as helping Albert, taking him to rest or getting adult/medical assistance if needed.

Sample story only. There is no single correct answer. Accept a sensible story and a reasonable ending.
Both clocks show a one-hour wait, from 3:00 to 4:00. The cause of Albert's discomfort is not stated.

朗读 1 · Happy Primary School

姓名：________________　日期：________________

Read the passage aloud. Then listen to the examiner and answer the questions orally.

7th January, 2012

Dear parents,

A programme to promote healthy eating will be started this month. We will check the list of the meals from the school's food producer. We hope that lunch boxes in this year have more healthy ingredients than those in the previous years. We would like to encourage students to pay more attention to what they eat.

Both students and parents have to know the importance of eating healthily. A lot of people think that healthy food doesn't taste good, so they choose to have fast food. However, having healthy eating habits can make us stronger. Therefore, we should encourage our students to build up healthy eating habits.

I hope you can prepare some healthy food at home as well. Let's create healthy eating habits with students together!

Yours sincerely,

Andy Wong

Principal, Andy Wong

口头提问 · Happy Primary School

请在学生完成朗读或讲述后逐题提问，不提前展示问题。

  1. Who wrote the school notice?
    Principal Andy Wong wrote the school notice.
  2. What is the name of the school?
    The school is called Happy Primary School.
  3. Why do people choose to have fast food?
    They choose to have fast food because they think healthy food doesn't taste good.
  4. In the sentence 'I hope you can prepare some healthy food at home as well', who does 'you' refer to?
    'You' refers to the parents.
  5. Do you have healthy eating habits? Why do you think so?
    Yes, I do. I eat fruit and vegetables every day, and I drink plenty of water.

Let the student read the passage aloud first. Keep these questions out of the student's view, then ask them orally.
Reference answers are for the examiner. Accept equivalent answers in the student's own words.
Question 5 is a personal response; the sample does not describe the student's actual habits.

朗读 3 · Angel's Blog

姓名：________________　日期：________________

Read the passage aloud. Then listen to the examiner and answer the questions orally.

Our class went on a short trip to the Sunny Island Holiday Village on Cheung Chau Island last weekend. I arrived at the ferry pier in Central at eight thirty in the morning. When Miss Ho and all my classmates turned up at nine, we got on the ferry. The ferry ride was nice. It took about half an hour to reach the island.

As soon as we got off the ferry, I saw a girl who was wearing a beautiful red dress and a hat. It was Angie, my cousin! What a surprise to run into her! She was there with my uncle and aunt to enjoy a relaxing holiday. Since the weather was hot and there was no shelter along the way, our class went by bus to the holiday village.

When we arrived, Miss Chan led us to the activity room and put us into groups. Miss Chan gave us our room keys. We put our bags into our rooms and went back to the activity room. Then we started to play some group games. Miss Chan wanted us to learn the importance of teamwork, so we played games like tug-of-war and did a three-legged race. After that, we had some free time to enjoy the facilities in the holiday village. I played mini-golf and went bowling with my friends. Before we left, we took a class photo with Miss Chan. It was a wonderful trip and I am already looking forward to another school trip!

口头提问 · Angel's Blog

请在学生完成朗读或讲述后逐题提问，不提前展示问题。

  1. Where did Angel and her classmates meet?
    They met at the ferry pier in Central.
  2. What time did Angel's class arrive on the island?
    They arrived at about half past nine in the morning.
  3. Who did Angel meet on the pier in Cheung Chau?
    She met her cousin Angie.
  4. Why did Angel's class take a bus to the holiday village?
    They took a bus because it was hot and there was no shelter along the way.
  5. What did Angel's class learn from the group games and activities?
    They learnt the importance of teamwork.
  6. What did Angel's class do before they left?
    They took a class photo with Miss Chan.

Let the student read the passage aloud first. Keep these questions out of the student's view, then ask them orally.
Reference answers are for the examiner. Accept equivalent answers in the student's own words.
The arrival time is about 9:30 a.m.: 9:00 departure plus about half an hour. Miss Ho and Miss Chan are both retained as printed.
