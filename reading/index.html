moderator noun

主持人

The moderator asked each child a question.

smartest adjective

最聪明的

Mia is the smartest student in the class.

smart 的最高级

gonna informal phrase

将要；打算

I'm gonna read a book tonight.

going to 的非正式口语形式，正式写作使用 going to

seriously adverb

认真地；严肃地

Dad spoke seriously about road safety.

Seriously? 也可表示“真的？”

punishment noun

惩罚；处罚

As a punishment, Tom could not watch TV that evening.

unfortunately adverb

不幸地；遗憾地

Unfortunately, my ice cream fell on the ground.

dictionary noun

词典；字典

I looked up the word in a dictionary.

forked adjective

分叉的；叉状的

The bird sat on a forked branch.

nickel noun

五分硬币（美国、加拿大等）

In the United States, a nickel is worth five cents.

也可指金属“镍”；此例句为硬币义

cornered adjective

被逼到角落的；陷入困境的

The cornered mouse had nowhere to run.

interrogated verb

审问；盘问

The detective interrogated the suspect about the missing jewels.

interrogate 的过去式／过去分词

nerd noun

对某领域很痴迷的人；书呆子（口语）

I'm a science nerd, and I love learning about space.

例句为友善的自我描述；称呼他人时可能不礼貌

worried adjective

担心的；忧虑的

I was worried about my lost dog.

resolutions noun

决心；决议

My New Year's resolutions are to read more and exercise every day.

沿用原词页；原手写辨字尚待确认；例句使用“新年决心”义

chewing verb

咀嚼

The boy is chewing a piece of apple.

chew 的现在分词／动名词

sugar noun

糖

Mum added a spoonful of sugar to the tea.

familiar adjective

熟悉的

That dog looks familiar to me.

liability noun

责任；负债

The company accepted liability for the broken window.

此例句使用“赔偿责任”义

scarcity noun

稀缺；不足

A scarcity of water made it hard to grow vegetables.

knowledgeable adjective

知识渊博的；了解的

Our teacher is knowledgeable about animals.

ability noun

能力

Her ability to ride a bike improved with practice.

tenancy noun

租期；租赁

Our tenancy of this flat lasts for one year.

指租用房产的期限或租赁关系

vacancy noun

空缺；空房

The hotel has a vacancy, so we can stay there tonight.

此例句使用“空房”义

availability noun

可获得性；可用性

We checked the availability of the book at the library.

即查看这本书是否有可借阅的馆藏

scary adjective

吓人的；可怕的

The ghost in the story was scary.

clarity noun

清楚；清晰度

My new glasses let me see everything with greater clarity.
